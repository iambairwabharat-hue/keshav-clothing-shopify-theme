Frequently Asked Questions

Orders & Payment

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay.

Since our drops are limited and we process orders quickly, we cannot guarantee changes or cancellations once an order is placed. Please email support immediately if you need assistance.

Your card will be charged immediately upon placing the order to secure your limited edition piece.

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking link. You can also view the status from your account page.

Shipping & Delivery

Yes, we ship globally. Shipping costs and delivery times are calculated at checkout based on your location.

Domestic orders typically take 3-5 business days. International orders can take 7-14 business days depending on customs.

For international orders, customers are responsible for any import duties and taxes charged by their local customs office.

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $99.00.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached. Archive sale items are final sale.

Contact our support team with your order number to initiate a return request and receive shipping instructions.

Due to the limited nature of our drops, we cannot guarantee stock for exchanges. We recommend returning the item and placing a new order.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

Sizing & Fit

Most of our pieces are designed with a modern, oversized fit. If you prefer a more standard fit, we recommend sizing down.

Detailed measurements can be found on our Size Guide page and on individual product pages.

Model sizing information is listed in the description of each product page for reference.

Product Care

We recommend machine washing cold inside out and laying flat to dry to preserve the heavyweight cotton and graphic prints.

Our garments are pre-washed and pre-shrunk, but minimal shrinkage may occur if exposed to high heat in the dryer.

Always wash and iron the garment inside out. Do not iron directly over the printed graphics.
